Solution to Challenge statement.
Our solution is a social productivity app that helps users reach their goals by putting something on the line. Most productivity apps rely on self-discipline, which is easy to ignore when no one is watching. Our app however adds real accountability by having users stake an embarrassing photo of themselves on each goal. If they fail to follow through with their goal that photo is posted to their friends feed. The idea is that the pressure of not wanting friends to see the photo and know they failed their goal pushes users to complete their goals consistently.


Project Vision
For people that want to be more productive who need help managing their goals, the Stake app is an accountability platform that allows users to “stake” embarrassing photos of themselves to highly motivate users to complete their goals. Unlike Habitify, which relies on reminders that are easy to ignore, our product creates social pressure by making users' progress visible to friends and a personal stake for each goal.
